Our Roots & Founder

Every Journey Begins With a Conversation

Biznaga began not as an agency, but as an enduring romance with Spain’s hidden corners. Founded by Casey after years spent living, wandering, and breaking bread alongside winemakers in La Rioja, flamenco dynasties in Triana, and ceramic masters in Seville, our approach is unapologetically personal.

We don’t curate tours from brochures or algorithms. Every relationship is cultivated through handshakes, shared bottles, and quiet back-alley recommendations that never find their way into mainstream guidebooks. When we design your journey, we open doors to the people who give Spain its pulse.
